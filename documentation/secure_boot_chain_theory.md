## Purpose
Explain trust establishment from firmware to OS and application layers in high-level terms.

## Key Ideas
- Each stage verifies the next stage before execution.
- Signatures and trusted keys anchor the chain of trust.
- Recovery/update processes must preserve integrity guarantees.

## Why It Matters Here
Security engineers need to understand platform trust assumptions even when working on application-layer vulnerability research and remediation.
